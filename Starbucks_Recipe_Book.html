Coffee base vs Crème base: same idea (sweetener + thickener), but the Crème base has no coffee so kids or decaf customers can have it. Frappuccinos have no Short or hot Venti. Check counts against your store's recipe cards.

Tip: whole milk steams best for latte art; oat (barista edition) is the best non-dairy foamer. Xanthan gum (¼ tsp) gives Frappuccinos the smooth, non-separating texture.
